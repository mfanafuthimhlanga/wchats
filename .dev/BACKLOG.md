## How to read the numbers (added 2026-08-12, because they were unreadable)

**The number is an address, not a priority and not a description.** It says *which section an item
was filed under*, which mostly records *which audit found it* — nothing about what it is or how
urgent it is. `4.1` is not more important than `5.11`, and `0.x` is not "do first".

Every row therefore now carries a **slug** beside its number: `5.1 · ops15-server-gap`. **Use the
slug in conversation; the number exists only as a stable anchor.** It cannot be renumbered, because
source comments in `agent.py`, `eval.py`, `red_team_probe.py`, `transactional/tools.py` and others
cite rows by number (`# BACKLOG 2.1`, `5.9`), and a reader following one of those must not land on an
unrelated row. That is also why closed rows are struck through rather than renumbered away.
