# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Non-technical small business owners (salons, realtors, repair shops, e-commerce) who need a customer service agent on their site without hiring a developer. They arrive stressed and skeptical, work on mid-range Windows laptops, often in bright rooms, and judge the product in the first 90 seconds. A secondary audience is the technical evaluator (a hired freelancer or savvy owner) who inspects the eval scores and red-team results before trusting the agent with customers.

## Product Purpose

W Chats takes a business's documents and produces a defensible customer service agent: grounded in the documents, scored by evals, and red-teamed before it goes live. Signup → ingest → deploy in under 30 minutes. Success looks like an owner who watched their agent get built, saw the pass rates, and embedded the widget with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Engineered, candid, unhurried. The interface should feel like a precision instrument that happens to be easy to operate — closer to a flight computer than a marketing site. Evidence over reassurance: numbers, pass rates, and logs are the brand's charisma. The voice is sentence case, plain, and never sells.

## Anti-references

- The previous direction: a photographic skyline background with translucent glass cards. Ambitious but not production-worthy; retired.
- Generic dark SaaS dashboards (indigo-on-navy, glowing gradients, hero metrics with sparkline confetti).
- SaaS-cream marketing pages and friendly-startup pastel illustration styles.
- Anything that reads "template": identical card grids, eyebrow labels above every section, gradient text.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence is the aesthetic.** Scores, counts, timestamps, and logs are set with typographic care — the data is the decoration.
2. **Ledger, not lounge.** Dense, aligned, mono-forward tables and rows; hairlines and alignment do the separating, not boxes-in-boxes.
3. **One cinematic moment.** The landing and auth surfaces get the single expressive three.js scene; every working screen is calm, instant, and content-first.
4. **The owner is not a developer.** Terminal precision in look, never in hostility: plain-language labels, obvious next actions, visible progress.
5. **Fast on a 4 GB laptop.** No heavy effects in the admin; motion is rare, purposeful, and respects reduced-motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, focus visible on every interactive element, full keyboard operability, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored everywhere (three.js scenes render a static frame). Mono numerals for scanning; never color as the only signal on status.
