# Kagiso Bakery: delivery

We deliver within 15 km of the shop. That covers Kagiso, Krugersdorp, Randfontein,
Azaadville, Munsieville and Rant-en-Dal. Orders further out can be collected from the shop
or sent by courier at the courier's rate, which we quote before you pay.

## Delivery fees

Delivery costs R35 for orders under R300 and is free for orders of R300 or more. Same-day
delivery is available for orders placed on WhatsApp before 10:00, for an extra R25. We do
not deliver on Sundays or public holidays.

## Delivery times

Weekday deliveries leave the shop at 11:00 and at 15:00. Saturday deliveries leave at 11:00
only. The driver phones you when he is ten minutes away. If nobody is home the order goes
back to the shop and can be collected until closing time the same day, after which it is
donated and no refund is given.

## Wedding and event orders

Orders over R2 000 are delivered by our own van at no charge anywhere in the West Rand,
and we set the cake up at the venue. Book at least fourteen days ahead.
