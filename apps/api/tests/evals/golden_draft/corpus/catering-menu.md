# Kagiso Bakery: catering menu

All catering prices are per person and include delivery within 15 km for orders of twenty
people or more. Book seven days ahead for anything under fifty people and fourteen days
ahead for larger events.

## Breakfast platters

The Morning Box is R65 per person and has two mini croissants, a muffin, a fruit skewer and
a 250 ml juice. The Builder's Breakfast is R85 per person and has a vetkoek with mince, a
boiled egg, a banana and a 500 ml Coke.

## Lunch platters

The Sandwich Platter is R70 per person: three half sandwiches, chicken mayo, cheese and
tomato, and ham and mustard. Vegetarian and halaal versions cost the same and must be asked
for when ordering. The Kota Platter is R60 per person and has one quarter kota with polony,
cheese, chips and atchar.

## Cakes for events

A 20 cm round sponge feeds twelve and costs R280. A 30 cm round feeds thirty and costs
R620. A three-tier wedding cake feeding 120 starts at R3 400 and needs a tasting, which is
free, at least three weeks before the wedding. Fondant decorations are R150 extra per tier.
