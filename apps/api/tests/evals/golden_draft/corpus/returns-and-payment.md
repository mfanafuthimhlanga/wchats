# Kagiso Bakery: payment and returns

## How to pay

We take cash, all bank cards, SnapScan and Zapper at the counter. For orders over R500 we
ask for a 50% deposit by EFT to Kagiso Bakery, FNB, account 62711 004 336, branch 250655,
with your order number as the reference. The balance is paid on collection or delivery.
We do not take cheques, and we do not offer credit accounts.

## If something is wrong with your order

Bring the product and your till slip back to the shop within 24 hours and we will replace
it or refund it, whichever you prefer. Because everything we sell is fresh, we cannot take
back an item after 24 hours or one that has been partly eaten, unless it is unsafe to eat.

For custom cakes, tell us about a problem on the day of collection. A cake that is not what
was ordered is remade at no charge if we have four hours, otherwise it is refunded in full.
A change of mind after the deposit is paid forfeits the deposit, because the ingredients
have already been bought.

## Loyalty card

Every R50 spent earns one stamp. Ten stamps earn a free 700 g loaf of white or brown bread.
Stamps expire after six months.
