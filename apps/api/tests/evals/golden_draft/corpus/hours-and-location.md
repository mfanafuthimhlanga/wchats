# Kagiso Bakery: hours and location

Kagiso Bakery is at 14 Marabastad Road, Kagiso, Krugersdorp, 1754. The shop is the blue
building beside the Engen garage, and there is free parking for six cars in front.

## Opening hours

We bake from 04:00 every day. The shop opens at 06:30 and closes at 17:00 from Monday to
Friday. On Saturdays we open at 06:30 and close at 14:00. On Sundays and public holidays we
open at 07:00 and close at 12:00. We are closed on Christmas Day and on 1 January.

Fresh bread comes out of the oven at 06:30, 09:00 and 12:00. Cakes and confectionery are
made to order and are ready for collection after 10:00 on the day agreed.

## Getting hold of us

The shop telephone is 011 410 2266 and is answered during opening hours. WhatsApp orders go
to 072 331 9040 and are read from 05:00. The email address for catering quotes is
orders@kagisobakery.co.za. We answer emails within one working day.
