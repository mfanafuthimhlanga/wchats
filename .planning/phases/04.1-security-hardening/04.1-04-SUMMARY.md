## What Was Built

### F5 — Redis Result Expiry (T-04.1-04-01, T-04.1-04-02)

Added `result_expires=300` to `celery_app.conf.update` in the Reliability section, after `task_reject_on_worker_lost`. Task results are now purged from the Redis result backend after 5 minutes, reducing the PII exposure window from indefinite to 5 minutes.

Created `LOCAL-DEV-REDIS.md` documenting the operational requirement to start Redis with `redis-server --save ""` in local dev, explaining why (Phase 04 task args now carry `body.message` — customer text up to 2000 chars). The `.env.example` `REDIS_SAVE_DISABLED` entry was already present from Plan 03.

### F6 — Soul Field Sanitisation at Admit-Time (T-04.1-04-03)

Added two `field_validator` methods to `AgentSoulUpdate` in `apps/api/app/schemas/agent.py`:

- `sanitise_text_field` — applied to `soul_voice` and `soul_role` (`mode="before"`, strips injection markers via `sanitize_chunk_text`)
- `sanitise_list_field` — applied to `soul_do_list` and `soul_donot_list` (`mode="before"`, maps `sanitize_chunk_text` over each list item)

The `name` field is intentionally excluded — it is a display field, not a soul field.

Import path confirmed as `app.utils.sanitize` (not `app.services.sanitize`).
