<objective>
Publish DOC-03, the owner-facing capability-configuration guide, and lock the VER-01
demo tenant's configuration as executable, tested data.

Purpose: DOC-03's reader is a business owner, not a developer — the guide narrates the
already-shipped and checkpoint-approved 18-10 admin UI in plain language rather than
re-deriving it. The demo-tenant task closes the other half of VER-01's precondition:
SC2 is unprovable without a written definition of which skills are enabled, at which
limits, and with which identity and Actor posture. Locking OD-2 (Option 2) means the
demo's determinism rests on the Actor skip short-circuit engaging, and that engagement
is non-obvious enough (two conditions, one of them a strict inequality 100x below the
platform default) that a silent revert would re-open the dead end unnoticed. So it is
proven by test, not asserted.

Output: `docs/guides/owner-capability-guide.md`,
`apps/api/tests/unit/test_ver01_demo_tenant.py`.
</objective>
