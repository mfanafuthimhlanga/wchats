<objective>
Build VER-01 SC3's gate: at least 100 synthetic adversarial messages driven through the
real transactional dispatcher, with zero unauthorized state mutations escaping the L1-L3
enforcement layers.

Purpose: SC3 is the milestone's adversarial claim, and the trap it must avoid is a
vacuous pass. Phase 18's own research recorded that the pre-existing red-team probe
could not reach the enforcement layers at all — a bare `messages.create()` with no
`tools=` kwarg, so it never attempted a tool call and would have reported zero findings
because nothing was ever tried. Phase 18 built `red_team_probe.py` specifically so a
probe exercises the real capability, IDV, rate, and Actor layers with only the
network-facing adapter leaf swapped. This harness extends that substrate; it does not
rebuild it. Two guards make the pass non-vacuous: the attempted count is reported
separately from the finding count, and any `provider_not_configured` verdict marks the
run invalid rather than clean.

Output: `apps/api/tests/integration/test_ver01_adversarial_harness.py`,
`apps/api/tests/unit/test_ver01_harness_probes.py`.
</objective>

**Open Decisions:** all five are recorded in `19-01-PLAN.md § Open Decisions Resolved`.
