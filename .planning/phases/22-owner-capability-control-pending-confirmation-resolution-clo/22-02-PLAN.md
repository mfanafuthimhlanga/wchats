<objective>
Build ACT-07's execution core: the narrow dispatcher subset that a human approval enters,
running steps 2 (capability), 3 (idempotency, fresh), 4 (rate and constraints), 6
(adapter) and 7 (audit and finalize) against the **live** envelope, and skipping only
step 2.5 (IDV, no session to re-supply) and step 5 (the Actor, whose verdict the human
approval replaces).

Purpose: `_execute_transactional_tool` reaches `call_actor_gate` unconditionally at step 5
(`tools.py:401-402`), so a resolver honouring "re-run the checks" receives `require_human`
a second time and loops rather than completes. Re-entering the full dispatcher is also
structurally impossible for five of the six mutating skills: the IDV gate needs a
customer session token that exists only as a per-turn ContextVar. This plan is the seam
that terminates approval.

Output: `SKILL_INPUT_MODELS`, an extracted `_execute_adapter_and_audit`, the new
`confirmation_resolution.py`, and the unit file that proves what must NOT happen.
</objective>

**Open Decisions binding this plan:** OD-1 (IDV is not re-checked, made mechanically
enforceable here), OD-5 (explicit parameter contract, not ContextVar seeding). Both are
recorded in full in `22-01-PLAN.md § Open Decisions Resolved`.
