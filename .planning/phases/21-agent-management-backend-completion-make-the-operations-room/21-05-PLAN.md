Operator grades are stored as append-only control-DB job_events rows (event_type='trace.graded') — no new bench table, honoring the RESEARCH "bench reads existing job_events+messages" guidance and making irrevocability a refuse-to-write invariant. This is a documented plan decision (see Assumptions).
