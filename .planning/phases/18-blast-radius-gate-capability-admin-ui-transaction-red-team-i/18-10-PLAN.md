## Design authority for this plan

Read in this order and treat each as binding:
1. **`DESIGN.md`** (repo root, rewritten 2026-07-26) — the shipped GOTHAM system. Its central law is that
   **colour is a verdict, never a decoration**; LIVE is brightness, not a hue; the gate "is not a badge, it
   is the room"; and its anti-pattern list explicitly names "a chip used as a category tag rather than a
   verdict".
2. **`18-UI-SPEC.md`** — decisions D1 through D6, the Copywriting Contract (every string is given
   verbatim; use those strings, do not paraphrase), the spacing table (literal shipped values, not
   rounded to an 8pt grid), the typography table, and the Structural placement section.
3. **`18-PATTERNS.md`** § the `deploy/page.tsx` entry — real `path:line` excerpts for `AppearanceTile`,
   the `saveWidgetConfig` mutation and its saving/saved stamp, the `useGate` derivation, and the
   auth-token fetch convention.

**D6 is a change from the researcher's first draft and is easy to get wrong:** in the acknowledgement
summary table, "Confirmation required" and "Verification required" render as plain `.help`-weight label
text present only when true, with the cell left empty when false. **They are not chips.** `ChipVerdict` is
a closed union (`live | pass | fail | seal | mute`) enforced by construction in `Chip.tsx`, and spending a
chip on a presence label dilutes exactly the verdict signal the owner most needs to read correctly at the
moment they accept financial risk.

**D5's rejected alternative is also load-bearing:** envelope drift must **not** be folded into the
`useGate()` call. `gateBlocked` is derived at `deploy/page.tsx:522` as
`checklistBlocked || redTeamBlockedSignal` and must **not** gain a third `||` term.
