## What to Watch For (agents home + agents/new)
Based on what was wrong on landing — expect same class of issues:

1. **Veil/darkness** — globals.css veil is now correct (0.45). But page-level components may have their own dark overlays or opaque backgrounds blocking the skyline. Remove those.
2. **Font color contrast** — Check `--text-2` (#C4BCD0) and `--text-3` (#8A82A0) elements against their actual backgrounds. Cards with `--surface-1`/`--surface-2` backgrounds should render token colors correctly. Cards without backgrounds may need text-shadow or a slightly stronger token.
3. **Glass card backgrounds** — Some cards may still have heavy `--glass-bg-strong` or solid `--surface-*` backgrounds where they should be lighter/transparent. Compare against reference.
4. **Inherited darkness** — Inner page wrappers or layout containers may have `background: var(--bg)` or `background: var(--bg-deep)` that block the skyline. These should be transparent on inner pages too unless specifically a card surface.
