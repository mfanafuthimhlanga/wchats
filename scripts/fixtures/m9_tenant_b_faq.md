# W Chats FAQ

## Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email. Check inbox for reset link.

## Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, and bank transfer. All cards must be 3D Secure enabled.

## Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard 3-5 business days. Express 1-2 business days. International 7-14 business days.

## Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement. Contact support immediately after that window.

## Q: How do I track my order?
A: Log in and go to Orders. Click the order number. A tracking link appears once the item ships.

## Q: What is the return policy?
A: Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.

## Q: How do I start a return?
A: Go to Orders in your account. Select the order. Click "Return Item" and follow the prompts.

## Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Refunds process in 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

## Q: Is there a loyalty program?
A: Yes. Every purchase earns points. 100 points equals $1 off future orders. Points expire after 12 months.

## Q: How do I redeem loyalty points?
A: Add items to cart. At checkout, click "Apply Points" and enter the amount you want to redeem.

## Q: Do you offer gift cards?
A: Yes. Gift cards are available in $10, $25, $50, and $100 denominations. They never expire.

## Q: How do I contact support?
A: Email support@example.com or call 1-800-555-0100. Hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm EST.

## Q: What are your support hours?
A: Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm EST. Weekend support via email only, response within 24 hours.

## Q: Can I change my delivery address?
A: Yes, if the order has not yet shipped. Contact support with your order number and new address.

## Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, to over 50 countries. International orders may be subject to customs duties.

## Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

## Q: Can I order without an account?
A: Yes, guest checkout is available. An account is recommended for order tracking and returns.

## Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click "Sign Up" at the top of any page. Enter your name, email, and a password. Verify your email.

## Q: I forgot my username. What do I do?
A: Your username is your email address. Use "Forgot Password" if you cannot log in.

## Q: How do I unsubscribe from emails?
A: Click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any marketing email. Allow 10 business days to take effect.

## Q: Do you price match?
A: We match prices from major authorized retailers. Submit a request within 14 days of purchase.

## Q: What browsers are supported?
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend keeping your browser updated for best performance.

## Q: How do I use a discount code?
A: Enter the code in the "Promo Code" field at checkout. Codes cannot be combined with other offers.

## Q: Can I use multiple promo codes at once?
A: No. Only one promotional code can be applied per order.

## Q: What if my item arrives damaged?
A: Take a photo and email it to support within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a replacement.

## Q: How do I check product availability?
A: Product availability is shown on each product page. Out-of-stock items show an estimated restock date.

## Q: Can I reserve out-of-stock items?
A: Yes. Click "Notify Me" on any out-of-stock product page to receive an email when it is available.

## Q: What is your warranty policy?
A: All products carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Extended warranties are available at checkout.

## Q: How do I register my product for warranty?
A: Visit the warranty registration page and enter your order number and product serial number.

## Q: Do you offer bulk or corporate pricing?
A: Yes. Contact corporate@example.com for orders over 50 units.
