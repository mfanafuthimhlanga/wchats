How it works

Four steps. One pipeline.
No code.

You answer questions about your business in plain language. The platform handles parsing, retrieval, evaluation, red teaming, and continuous monitoring.

Step 01

Drop your data

PDFs, sheets, websites, FAQs. Structure-aware parsing keeps tables, lists and headings intact.

Step 02

Shape the soul

Name your agent. Describe its role and tone. The retrieval strategy is synthesised from your corpus shape.

Step 03

Test in sandbox

A generated eval suite runs against your agent. Pass rates, faithfulness, latency and cost surface as you watch.

Step 04

Deploy as iframe

Pre-deployment checklist clears. You get a snippet. Paste it on your site. Weekly red team and drift detection run automatically.

Agents that earn
the right to ship.

Most chatbot platforms hand you a widget and assume you have engineers waiting in the wings. W Chats handles what those engineers would do — and then it keeps doing it, after the agent is live, on your behalf.

Pre-deployment checklist
Eval pass rates, red team severity, latency, cost and corpus coverage all clear before the deploy button is even live.
Per-tenant data isolation
Dedicated Neon project per business. No shared vector store. Eval branches off live data without duplicating it.
Continuous post-deploy monitoring
Scheduled evals, weekly red team runs, monthly graph-RAG insights digest. You get a weekly brief, not a pager.
Verified knowledge layer
Eval-passing answers and approved production responses cache as canonical Q&A. Cost per query drops 40%+ by month three.
